# Welcome Try-On v6

Generated from scratch with the built-in image generation tool. The resulting
portrait image was encoded as WebP for the mobile bundle. The SelfX logo and
welcome text remain Flutter overlays.

## Generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: an ORIGINAL full-bleed 9:16 portrait
photographic background for the welcome screen of a premium mobile virtual
fashion try-on app. Build a visually rich fashion moodboard/collage, inspired by
editorial catalog contact sheets: many large portrait photo tiles with slightly
varied rotations and rounded corners, separated by narrow near-black charcoal
gutters. Alternate between clean garment-only catalog photos (tailored cobalt
blazer, ivory blouse, burgundy dress, dark denim jacket, cream trousers) and
diverse adult people wearing coordinated finished outfits, so the relationship
between a clothing catalog and virtual try-on is immediately obvious. A few
adjacent garment/person tile pairs may hint at before-and-after styling. No
central couple or hero portrait; the collage itself is the image. Photographic
realism, luxurious studio and boutique settings within each tile, tasteful warm
copper and deep teal accents, varied male and female looks, premium polished
fashion campaign quality. Reserve the TOP-LEFT area covering about 42% of width
and 18% of height as a soft warm off-white light patch blended naturally into
the collage, entirely empty of photos or marks, so a separate orange logo can
be overlaid clearly. The rest of the top and middle should reveal the collage.
Gradually darken and simplify the LOWER 38% into deep charcoal while allowing
only subtle low-contrast glimpses of collage, because large white headline,
description, and orange button will be overlaid there. The background must
itself have no logo, no words, no letters, no UI controls, no phone frame, no QR
codes, no watermarks. This is a new image from scratch, not an edit or
recreation of any previous two-model boutique photo.
